SRE for a Global Mobile E-commerce Platform

Derived from Altimetrik (Dec 2024 - Present)

I helped maintain 24x7 availability for a high-traffic mobile e-commerce site. We implemented synthetic checks for critical flows including search, cart, and checkout, consolidated telemetry into one-view dashboards, and tuned Dynatrace problem rules to improve triage.

  • Reduced false positives through alert tuning and SLO-aware rules.
  • Added runbooks and incident templates integrated with Jira.
  • Performed deep dives across Kubernetes, SAP Hybris, Dynatrace, and OpenSearch.
