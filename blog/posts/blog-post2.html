SRE for a Global Mobile E‑commerce Platform

Derived from Altimetrik (Dec 2024 – Present)

I helped maintain 24×7 availability for a high‑traffic mobile e‑commerce site. We implemented synthetic checks for critical flows (search, cart, checkout), consolidated telemetry into one‑view dashboards (Grafana/Kibana/Firebase), and tuned Dynatrace problem rules to improve triage.

  • Reduced false positives by alert tuning and SLO‑aware rules.
  • Added runbooks and incident templates integrated with Jira.
  • Deep dives across Kubernetes (SAP Hybris), Dynatrace, and OpenSearch.
