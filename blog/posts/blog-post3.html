Monitoring at Scale with Dynatrace & Prometheus

Derived from AVASOFT (Observability SME)

For supply‑chain and retail clients, we unified Golden Signals across AKS/OpenShift with Dynatrace and Prometheus exporters. Standard dashboards and alert routes cut MTTR and enabled better on‑call focus.

  • Dashboards for Kafka, RabbitMQ, HiveMQ, and app latency/error budgets.
  • Alert routing to ITSM (ServiceNow/Jira) with noise suppression.
  • Trade‑offs: SaaS vs self‑hosted monitoring for data gravity and cost.
