Monitoring at Scale with Dynatrace & Prometheus

Derived from AVASOFT (Observability SME)

For supply-chain and retail clients, we unified golden signals across AKS and OpenShift with Dynatrace and Prometheus exporters. Standard dashboards and alert routes reduced MTTR and helped on-call teams focus on actionable signals.

  • Dashboards for Kafka, RabbitMQ, HiveMQ, app latency, and error budgets.
  • Alert routing to ITSM tools such as ServiceNow and Jira with noise suppression.
  • Tradeoffs between SaaS and self-hosted monitoring for data gravity and cost.
